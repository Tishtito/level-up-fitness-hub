# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Nairobi and the wider Kenyan market who want one credible place to discover training programs, compare memberships, book nutrition or physiotherapy support, and buy fitness products. They arrive with different levels of fitness experience and need to understand the offer, trust the specialists, and choose a useful next step without deciphering a complicated platform.

## Product Purpose

Level Up Fitness is a public fitness marketplace that connects training, coaching, nutrition, recovery, memberships, and equipment. The index page is the primary brand surface. It should turn a broad service catalogue into a coherent path from aspiration to action while making the quality of the people and care behind each offer visible.

## Brand Personality

Purposeful, aspirational, and holistic. The voice is grounded in three promises: “Transform Your Body,” “Elevate Your Life,” and “Care beyond the workout.” It should feel expert and encouraging without becoming aggressive, clinical, or performatively luxurious.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic purple SaaS landing-page patterns, repetitive equal-card grids, neon gym aggression, stock-photo transformation clichés, decorative glassmorphism, and interfaces that make every service compete at the same visual volume. Do not imitate Core Atelier’s identity; use its editorial pacing, confident whitespace, image-led storytelling, and direct booking paths as compositional references.

## Design Principles

- Lead with human outcomes, then explain the service that produces them.
- Treat training, nutrition, and recovery as one connected care journey.
- Make expertise and real evidence more prominent than decorative claims.
- Give each screen one unmistakable next action while preserving paths for exploration.
- Use restraint to signal confidence: fewer containers, stronger imagery, clearer hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard access, visible focus, readable text contrast, meaningful image alternatives, zoom and reflow at 200%, reduced-motion behavior, non-color status cues, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels where practical. Fitness imagery and language must represent varied bodies, ages, abilities, and starting points without shame-based framing.
